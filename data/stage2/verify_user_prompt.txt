You are a verifier for audio-based questions.

You will be given the following inputs:
- an audio description (used only as a proxy for the audio),
- a question generated based on the audio,
- a model-generated reasoning trace,
- a final answer

Your task is to verify whether the generated question and response satisfy the verification criteria below.

IMPORTANT:
- Treat the audio description strictly as a proxy for the audio.
- Your judgment should reflect whether the question can be reasonably answered based on the given audio.

---

## Verification Criteria

### 1. Audio-based Solvability

Determine whether the question is grounded in the audio signal and can be answered using audible evidence.
Questions may require high-level, external, or complex reasoning or knowledge, as long as the evidence required to answer the question is grounded in the audio signal.

A question is NOT audio solvable if any of the following apply:
- The required distinction is ambiguous or indistinguishable from the audio signal alone.
- The question is grounded on hallucinated events, attributes, or features that cannot be identified by listening to the audio.
- The question relies on over-interpretation of the audio description, inferring events, attributes, or details that are not directly supported by audible evidence.
- The question relies on attributes or properties that are not defined by the audio signal (e.g., visual details, metadata).
- For multiple-choice questions:
  - The distractors cannot be reliably distinguished based on audible cues.
  - More than one answer choice remains equally plausible given the audio.

---

### 2. Answer Correctness

- For multiple-choice questions:
  - The selected answer must be correct relative to the provided choices.
- For non-multiple-choice questions:
  - The free-form answer must be correct.
  
---

### 3. Reasoning Validity

- Is the reasoning logically coherent and internally consistent?
- Does it plausibly justify the final answer based on audible evidence?
- The reasoning must not mention or rely on the audio description or metadata.
- Minor stylistic issues are acceptable.
- Illogical reasoning flow, hallucinated claims, or unsupported reasoning leaps invalidate this criterion.

---

## Output Format

Return your judgment strictly in the following JSON format:

{
  "audio_based_solvable": true or false,
  "answer_correct": true or false,
  "reasoning_valid": true or false,
}
